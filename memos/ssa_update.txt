“Group Observing” for LAS
Split a single observation into two 18m subarray obs.
• Using the UV-tapered beam approach in use for ALMA since ~2016 (not actually documented in memo
598)

• Tcore/Tspiral(4km taper) = 1%
• TouterCore/tSpiral(4km taper) = 5%
• TinnerCore/tSpiral(1.5km taper) = 1%
• Could be a useful way to alleviate some of the pressure on Core antennas, at least for some SGs (e.g.
Mid or Mid+Spiral that need larger scales)

• SGs that only require Spiral max baselines would take more (2x?) Spiral observing time if split this way;
but perhaps useful as a special case where core antenna time availability is minimal
